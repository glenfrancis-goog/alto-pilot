# Annual Leave and Vacation Policy

## 1. Accrual Rates (Per Calendar Year)
* **Tenure 0 to 2 Years**: Fourteen (14) business days per annum.
* **Tenure 3 to 5 Years**: Eighteen (18) business days per annum.
* **Tenure 5+ Years**: Twenty-one (21) business days per annum.

## 2. Rules & Restrictions
* **Probationary Employees**: May not take more than three (3) consecutive days of annual leave during their initial 90-day probationary period without manager authorization.
* **Rollover Cap**: A maximum of five (5) unused annual leave days may be rolled over into the following calendar year. All excess accrued days expire on December 31 without cash encashment.
* **Working Day Definition**: Annual leave is deducted in business days (Monday through Friday), strictly excluding weekends and officially gazetted national public holidays.
