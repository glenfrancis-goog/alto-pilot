# Parental Leave Policy

## 1. Eligibility & Scope
* **Eligible Employee Types**: Regular Full-Time Employees (FTE) only.
* **Ineligible Classifications**: Third-party contractors, temporary agency workers, vendor personnel, and interns are strictly ineligible for company-funded parental leave.
* **Tenure Requirement**: Employees must have completed a minimum of ninety (90) continuous calendar days of service prior to the expected delivery or adoption date. Employees currently on ninety-day probationary status cannot take paid parental leave until probation is successfully completed.

## 2. Benefit Tiers
* **Primary Caregiver**: Entitled to sixteen (16) weeks of 100% paid leave.
* **Secondary Caregiver**: Entitled to four (4) weeks of 100% paid leave.
* **Usage Window**: Leave must be taken within twelve (12) months following birth or formal adoption placement.

## 3. Exceptions & Escalation Rules
* Any request exceeding statutory or standard caps (e.g. medical complications or postpartum health extensions) cannot be auto-approved by digital systems.
* All exception requests must be formally routed to the **Human Resources Business Partner (HRBP)** for medical documentation review and executive sign-off.
