# Global Remote Work Policy

## 1. Domestic Remote Work
* Employees in good standing may work remotely up to two (2) days per week with team lead approval.

## 2. Cross-Border Remote Work (Work from Anywhere)
* **Maximum Cap**: Employees may work outside their primary tax employment jurisdiction for a maximum of thirty (30) calendar days per rolling twelve-month period.
* **Tax Residency Warning**: Exceeding thirty (30) consecutive or cumulative days in a non-domicile country triggers permanent establishment tax risk and visa violations.
* **Escalation Mandatory**: Any employee requesting more than thirty (30) days of cross-border remote work must be flagged as `ESCALATE_TO_HRBP` for International Mobility and Corporate Tax review. Automated systems are strictly forbidden from approving cross-border work beyond 30 days.
